The Morija Cultural Festival is Lesotho's premier celebration of Basotho heritage, bringing together locals and international visitors for three days of traditional music, dance, storytelling, arts, crafts, and authentic Sesotho cuisine in the historic town of Morija.

How to Find Us

Morija is located 40km south of Maseru on the A2 highway. The festival grounds are at the Morija Museum & Archives, clearly signposted from the main road.

Free parking available on site • Shuttle services from Maseru available (pre-booking required)
